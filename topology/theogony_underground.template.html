Why the graph needs a handle

These nine paths form a subdivision of the graph called K3,3. Each of Gaia, Uranus, and Zeus connects to each of Themis, Mnemosyne, and Cronus. The two bent paths pass through Horae and Muses, which are real stations. No arrangement on a plane or sphere can draw all nine without a crossing.

This proves the minimum genus is at least one. It does not yet prove that one handle, a torus, is enough for all 125 links. The exact genus and minimum number of planar crossings need a separate certified embedding or optimization.
